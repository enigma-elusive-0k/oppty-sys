# Cover letter skeleton

200 to 250 words. Governed by `modules/VOICE_GUIDE.md` 4.1. The bracketed prompts are for the assistant; none of them survive into the letter.

---

[Salutation. Hiring manager's name if known; "Hello [Team] hiring team" if not. Never "To whom it may concern."]

[Recognition of the problem, 2 to 3 sentences. What specifically about this role or company caught the candidate's attention, framed as an understanding of what the employer is trying to do. Must fail the competitor-substitution test. No "I am excited to apply."]

[Relevance, 1 to 2 sentences. Why that problem is familiar: the recurring theme in the candidate's work that maps to it.]

[Evidence, 2 or 3 proof points. Each: situation, mechanism, outcome. Metrics where the master supports them at Level A or B. Fewer proof points carrying more weight; no capability lists.]

[Concern, 1 to 2 sentences, only if the reader will otherwise wonder. Level change, domain change, gap. Name it and state the mechanism that transfers.]

[Contribution close, 1 to 2 sentences. What the candidate wants to build or fix here. No request for an interview; no enthusiasm.]

[Sign-off. Name. Phone and email on one line if the letter is a standalone file.]

---

## Before it ships

- Employer in the first sentence.
- Every proof point traced (Step 5 format).
- Word count 200 to 250.
- Forbidden-phrase scan and em-dash scan clean.
- Reads in the candidate's register.
- Candidate has read it.
